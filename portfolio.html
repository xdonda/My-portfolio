THEMBEKA XULU

SPORTS COACH & BUSINESS WOMAN

I'm a passionate young woman born and raised in KZN. I grew up and went to school in Eshowe, and I'm now based in Ulundi. I fell in love with athletics as a young girl and have pursued it ever since. I started as an athlete and today I am a fitness coach. My goal is to inspire young people to never give up on their dreams.

More about my journey
My journey started on the track. I wanted to be a successful athlete, but I also wanted stability for my future. When running didn't feel like a sustainable career on its own, I decided to continue studying. I went to TVET College and studied Business Management, then learned basic coding (HTML5 and CSS3). Because I was an athlete, I wanted to understand the science behind speed and training, so I studied Physical Fitness online — reading every single page of my coaching manual.
That curiosity led me to become both a coach and a businesswoman.

My Vision

I want to live a life that if it isn't perfect, I still have no regrets. I want to wake up every day knowing I will make someone's day a little happier.

I founded Ubunye Fitness Youth Program NPC to make an impact and change the lives of young people around me. When I realized a nonprofit alone couldn't sustain itself, I had to think outside the box and started Fitness Crib.

My vision is not just to serve my local community, but to use all the skills and qualifications I've gained throughout my life to give the best of myself to more people — including adults who train with Fitness Crib.

Another thing I am most proud of is that I wrote the code for this personal portfolio and for the companies I founded.
